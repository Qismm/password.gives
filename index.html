WHY RANDOM PASSWORDS

What makes a strong password

Unique, random passwords are your best defense. Humans are bad at creating them — a generator handles it for you.
Strong passwords
Unique and random. Mix upper/lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols so they’re hard to guess or crack.
Why unique matters
Reused passwords mean one breach can unlock many accounts. Generate a different password for every service.
Random works
Random strings resist brute-force and dictionary attacks. Avoid birthdays, names, and common patterns.
No thinking required
Don’t invent passwords yourself. Generate one, copy it, and store it in a password manager so you only remember one master password.
